Omnisend is an email & SMS marketing plugin for WooCommerce, used by more than 150,000 ecommerce brands worldwide. Build your email and SMS lists, send targeted campaigns, create automation workflows, and see what each one earned — all from within your WordPress dashboard. Built-in AI writes your copy and builds your segments on every plan, and with Omnisend MCP, your ChatGPT or Claude can do the same from a chat window. Free to start, no credit card needed.

Some of the features of Omnisend include:

**🎉 All-in-one email & SMS marketing solution**

Create and send email and SMS campaigns to targeted audiences, onboard new customers with sequential emails, and automate your marketing in a visual workflow builder. Automations are roughly 2% of all email sends but drive close to a third of email revenue, so the pre-built flows are the fastest thing to switch on.
Once a campaign goes out, you get reports on open rate, click rate, and the sales that campaign generated. You can see who opened and who clicked, then segment further on that behavior.

**📧📱 Email & SMS Campaigns**

* Build email & SMS campaigns in a drag-and-drop editor, with 250+ ecommerce templates to start from
* Use dynamic content to personalize the experience for your customers
* Import your store's branding (logos, colors, and fonts) into your emails and popups automatically with Brand Assets AI
* Send or schedule emails and SMS for a future date and time, and track your results in real time
* Segment and target campaigns on behavior, demographics, purchase history, and more
* Set up campaigns that trigger on customer behavior: welcome new subscribers, recover abandoned carts, upsell and cross-sell
* Resend to non-openers with a fresh subject line using Campaign Booster
* Test different subject lines, content, and designs to see what works best for your audience
* Get detailed reporting on how many people opened, clicked, and converted from your emails

**⚙️ Automation workflows**

* Build powerful automation workflows in a visual builder, no code needed
* Automate everything from welcome emails to abandoned cart reminders and win-back campaigns
* Trigger workflows on user actions like sign-up, form submission, purchase, or inactivity
* Measure metrics at every step and find where you can do better
* Combine email, SMS, and push notifications in a single workflow
* Sync your email and SMS audiences with Facebook Custom Audiences and Google Ads
* Create advanced journeys with split paths, delays, and conditional logic in one automation
* Use dynamic content to show different product recommendations, pricing, and messaging to different segments

**📱 SMS marketing**

* Set up automated SMS campaigns for order confirmations, shipping updates, and promotions, from $0.007 per message
* Segment and target SMS on behavior, purchase history, and lifecycle stage
* Use customizable opt-in forms to collect phone numbers and consent to send SMS messages
* Stay compliant by default with frequency capping, localized quiet hours, and TCPA-friendly forms

**📝 Integrated opt-in forms**

* Create popups, flyouts, embedded forms, and landing pages right from Omnisend
* Describe the form you want and Forms AI Assistant builds the layout, copy, and design (paid plans)
* Use forms as automation triggers, and segment contacts by which form they filled out

**📊 Built-in analytics & dashboards**

* View your business insights from the Omnisend dashboard with graphs, charts, and data widgets
* Track revenue attribution by campaign, automation, and channel, with 18 months of history on Pro

**🤖 Omnisend AI — on every plan, free included**

* Generate email copy, subject lines, and preheaders in your brand's voice
* Get suggestions for grammar, tone, and style while you edit
* Describe an audience in plain words and AI builds the segment for you
* Recommend the right products for each shopper using browsing and purchase history
* Adapt one campaign to different customer types — no need to create multiples
* Group customers by recency, frequency, and spend with Customer Breakdown
* Spot who's about to churn and who your highest-value shoppers are

**🧠 Omnisend MCP: connect ChatGPT or Claude**

* Connect Omnisend to ChatGPT or Claude, then manage it right from the chat you already use
* Build segments and draft campaigns straight from the conversation
* Ask how your campaigns performed, which automations earned the most, and why a number moved

**🛒 Ecommerce integrations**

* Integrations: Omnisend works with 250+ ecommerce tools, including Gorgias, Yotpo, Zapier, and ShipStation
* Real-time sync: Customer, order, and product data updates instantly, so your segments are never stale

= WooCommerce integration: =

Omnisend installs as a WooCommerce plugin, so you can skip the third-party connector and the developer time that usually comes with it. Customer, order, and product data lands in your account in real time, which means you can segment contacts on purchase history, items bought, total spend, how recently they ordered, and send campaigns that match – abandoned cart reminders, post-purchase follow-ups, and replenishment. Product blocks pull live prices and stock from your catalog, so you're never updating an email by hand.

== FAQ ==

= Installation instructions =
Here are a few simple steps to start selling more with Omnisend:
1. Create your Omnisend account [here](https://app.omnisend.com/registrationv2/?utm_source=woocommerce_plugin&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=app_listing). Enjoy all features on our Free Plan
2. Download the Omnisend plugin
3. Install the Omnisend plugin and follow the instructions to set it up

= What are the technical requirements for the plugin installation? =
*   PHP 7.1+
*   WordPress 4.7.0+
*   WooCommerce plugin 6.0+
*   Omnisend account

= How can I reach Omnisend support? =
We give you stellar support and helpful advice anytime you need it. Check out our [Knowledge Base](https://support.omnisend.com/), shoot us an email support@omnisend.com, or just write us a direct message on our platform. We're available 24/7 for all users—both on Free and paid plans.

= Where can I find Omnisend Terms? =
Read Omnisend [Terms of Use](https://www.omnisend.com/terms)

= Can I use Omnisend with ChatGPT or Claude? =
Yes. [Omnisend is in the ChatGPT plugins list and an official connector in Claude's directory](https://www.omnisend.com/ai/mcp/), so there's no custom setup. Once connected, you can ask how campaigns performed, get segment suggestions, and create campaigns from the conversation. Any AI tool that supports MCP can connect using the Omnisend MCP server.

= I'm switching from Mailchimp or Klaviyo. Do I have to move everything myself? =
No. Give us access and [our team rebuilds your lists, flows, and templates](https://www.omnisend.com/migration/) within five days, free. That includes your custom automations, not just the standard ones. Self-serve migration tools are there if you'd rather do it yourself.

= How can I report security bugs? =
You can report security bugs through the Patchstack Vulnerability Disclosure Program. The Patchstack team help validate, triage and handle any security vulnerabilities. [Report a security vulnerability.](https://patchstack.com/database/vdp/omnisend-connect)
