=== Ion Peptide Contact Form ===
Version: 1.0.0

Shortcode:
[ion_contact_form]

Features:
- First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Order Number, Message, and agreement checkbox.
- US phone field with +1 default.
- Invisible Google reCAPTCHA v3 using the same hardcoded keys used by the other Ion forms.
- No visible reCAPTCHA block.
- Honeypot protection.
- Temporary/disposable email blocking.
- Spam and unsafe-content checks.
- Rate limiting by IP and email.
- Saves submissions in WP Admin > Contact Requests.
- Dashboard shows reCAPTCHA Status and email delivery statuses.
- Admin notification goes to Support@ionpeptide.com.
- Customer confirmation goes to the submitted email address.
- AJAX submission with inline errors and confirmation, no page redirect.
